Silla-Tierra

Book 3 of the mysterious wheelchair

Chapter 5: Zuphixy and Poupleten can't find Zoofixy Jr.

It was late in the day when Zuphixy got home from his job at the Hospital. When he got home he learned from Poupleten that their son was not home yet. As it was close to midnight, they decided to wait until the next day to look for him. They assumed he stayed at a friend's place. Little did they know he was really in Silla-Tierra with his grandfather. They still didn’t know where the grandfather was.

When their son was still not home the next day, they phoned around to see where he was. Their phone calls made more people wonder where he was. Everyone was surprised that there were no reports of him doing anything to cause trouble. Now the police would not help. They did not want to waste their time looking for him. The police chief remembered how Zuphixy had shown up mid-search nineteen years ago. He was scared that Zoofixy Jr. would do the same.

At the hospital the staff were a bit confused about what happened to one of their wheelchairs. They didn't want to say it disappeared yet because they thought that it would be in just another part of a hospital. After some time they reported it missing to Zuphixy.

All this was starting to stress Zuphixy out. He was stressed because now not only was his father, Mr. Quzefuly missing, but now his son, Zoofixy Jr. was missing as well! The hospital administrator was getting mad that wheelchairs were disappearing and blamed Zuphixy.

Zuphixy was looking for a way to get the hospital administrator not to be mad at him, so he went to the local wheelchair store to see if they could help him out. Now the sales men thought that Zuphixy was looking for a wheelchair for his own personal use, and had him sit in some to test the chairs out. At first Zuphixy didn’t want to sit in any of the chairs, but he was feeling pressured. He tried to explain himself but when he realized the sales men were not understanding him he decided to do what they asked. Just as Zuphixy sat in a chair he vanished. Now as you will know if you read the book called, “How many mysterious wheelchairs are there” you would know that this was not the first time someone vanished from the store.

At the same time Purpleton had gone to the hospital. She went there because the stress of everyone disappearing had given her early labor. She was expecting to have a baby girl in one month. The hospital staff insisted that she sit in a wheelchair. The hospital staff had to call Zuphixy in to work to do the C-section. Seeing as he had vanished from the wheelchair store, they could not get a hold of him. Just as Purpleton sat in a wheelchair and she vanished as well! The hospital was left there with an empty wheelchair.
