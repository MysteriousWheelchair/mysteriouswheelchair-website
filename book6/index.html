Wilsons Go Traveling Through Obstacles

About the Story

When the Wilson family boards a cruise ship, they hope for a week of sunshine and calm seas. But traveling with six‑year‑old Elijah, who has autism, means every new sound, crowd, and rule can turn into a storm. Across the deck, Zuphixy Van Dijk — a young man from the Netherlands with cerebral palsy — faces his own obstacles as he learns that not every port is accessible and not every kindness is easy to accept.

As their paths cross, misunderstandings, meltdowns, and moments of compassion reveal how fragile and strong people can be at the same time. From chaotic check‑ins to quiet acts of empathy, The Wilsons Go: Traveling Through Obstacles is a moving story about families who keep going — even when the world isn’t built for them.
