How Many Mysterious Wheelchairs are there?

About the Story

In this second adventure in Silla‑Tierra, the living wheelchairs need help once again — but this time, the boy’s mother is the first to arrive in their world. When danger rises, the boy, now grown into an adult, must return to the land he once saved. Old friends, new challenges, and deeper mysteries await as he steps back into a place he thought he had left behind.
