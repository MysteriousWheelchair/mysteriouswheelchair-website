Silla-Tierra

Book 3 of the mysterious wheelchair

About the Story

Zuphixy's family journey continues as the mysterious wheelchair carries them back to Silla‑Tierra — a city built on hope and heart. But danger has arrived in the form of the Ear Men, whose plan to erase the city’s voice threatens everything that makes it alive. To save Silla‑Tierra, the family must uncover forgotten truths, face impossible choices, and learn that courage sometimes speaks louder than fear. Book 3 deepens the mystery and magic of the series, blending adventure, emotion, and the power of connection.
