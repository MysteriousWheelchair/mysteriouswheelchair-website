Chapter 7: Everyone is Together Again

Miss. Quzefuly and Zuphixy found themselves outside of their place in the human world again. At first they thought it was just the two of them that made it back to this world. But then they saw a woman who was dressed up in all purple with a teen boy who, according to Miss. Quzefuly, looked a lot like Zuphixy had looked when he was a teenager. Then the women stopped in front of their place and said “Hi” to them and introduced herself as Purpleten and her son as Zoofixy Jr.

Zuphixy was unsure if it was the magic, or if it was just someone who read his book about Purpleten. So he asked them a bunch of things in the questionnaire that would prove that they really were Purpleten and her son. Purpleten gave Zuphixy more proof than he had asked for. Zuphixy and Miss. Quzefuly were fully convinced Purpleten and her son had to be who they had said they were.Everyone went into the house and it was decided, as not to scare Zuphixy’s father too badly, they would go in and let him know that they were back again from Silla-Tierra.

Mr. Quzefuly was crying in his bedroom because he thought that he lost both his wife and his son. When suddenly he felt a hand on his shoulder, and his wife's voice said in a soft caring voice “why are you crying?”. Now at first Mr. Quzefuly thought that he had only imagined his wife voice, so he did not responded. But then his wife said “Are you crying because you thought that I had died like we thought are son had done when he was sixteen?”

Then Zuphixy sat in front of his father and said “Don’t be sad! We are here with you again. Just like when I was sixteen I found myself in that land again, but this time I found my mother there too”.

Mr. Quzefuly said “That can not be as we told you back then; there is no such thing as magically going to another world. Right Miss. Quzefuly?”

Miss. Quzefuly said “Um well… that was what I was lead to believe until it happened. But everything that Zuphixy just told you is true. You see, I met his son who now is a teenager and his wife in that other world and I experienced things there that Zuphixy never told me about.”

Mr. Quzefuly said “I do not believe you! I think you ran away and made up this story of another world so that you wouldn’t have to tell me where you really have been for the last two weeks!”

Zuphixy showed his father some photos of Purpleten and Zoofixy that he had taken while he was Silla-Tierra. Now Zuphixy saw them the way they looked in Silla-Tierra and Mr. Quzefuly saw them like normal people and said “Oh so this is your friend from university?” Just then a woman dressed in all purple walked in, and with her there was a teenage boy who looked a lot like Zuphixy when he was a teenager. Miss. Quzefuly said “Oh, hi Purpleten and Zoofixy. This is my husband and Zuphixy’s father.”

Now Mr. Quzefuly was shocked at how much the boy looked like his son that he suddenly said “I think I am having a heart attack!” His wife had to call EMS and he ended up going to the hospital. Purpleten and Zoofixy Jr. were not allowed to visit him as they were not known to be family by Mr. Quzefuly.

After Mr. Quzefuly recovered in the hospital for a week, he came home and they tried again to let him know that Purpleten and Zoofixy Jr. were part of the family. This time he was not as shocked, but he still did not believe that there was a world called Silla-Tierra. Mr. Quzefuly said that he believed that his wife had gone under cover to find a female friend for Zuphixy. Mr. Quzefuly believed that she came out of hiding only when Zuphixy agreed to be a boy friend to the women. He also thought that Zuphixy and Miss. Quzefuly had said that they were in Silla-Tierra as a way to explain why Zuphixy had not been able to find Miss. Quzefuly until that day he had his heart attack.
