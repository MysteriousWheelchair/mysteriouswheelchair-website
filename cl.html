The Mysterious Wheelchair

Character List

Main Characters

  • Zuphixy — a 16‑year‑old boy with golden hair.
  • Zuphixy’s Father — a 40‑year‑old man who works as a computer technician.
  • Zuphixy’s Mother — a 37‑year‑old woman who works as a domestic engineer.
  • Zuphixy’s Guide Dog — a golden retriever who assists him.
  • Purpletten — a 10‑year‑old girl who is a living wheelchair.
  • Purpletten’s Father — a 40‑year‑old red wheelchair.
  • Purpletten’s Mother — a 38‑year‑old blue wheelchair.
  • Purpletten’s Uncle — a living blue‑and‑green wheelchair whose job is detective.
  • Mr. Oidohombre — a man made entirely of ears; he is deaf and the villain.

Extended Characters

  • Living Wheelchair Police — striped wheelchairs who serve as law‑enforcement.
  • Real‑World Police — human police officers who appear in the story.
  • Zoofixy — Zuphixy’s son, whose age keeps changing throughout the story.
